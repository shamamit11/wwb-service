# SEO Knowledge

## SEO Principles

- public content should expose accurate, stable metadata
- AI-generated SEO suggestions should remain editable
- SEO automation must support editorial override

## Likely SEO Concerns

- titles and meta descriptions
- slugs and canonical URLs
- structured data
- internal linking support
- sitemap correctness

## Editorial Rule

SEO suggestions derived from AI are inputs to review, not direct publish actions.
