# Skill: Knowledge Base

## Purpose

Use this skill for reusable research, notes, experience records, references, and post-supporting knowledge workflows.

## Context To Load

- `.agent/knowledge-base/module-map.md`
- `.agent/knowledge-base/content-lifecycle.md`
- `docs/KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md`

## Use This When

- adding knowledge item CRUD
- designing search, tagging, or categorization
- linking knowledge items to posts or topics
- evaluating whether content pipelines preserve originality

## Product Rules

- knowledge items support better writing and better AI assistance
- they are not automatically public content
- quality and provenance matter more than volume
- links to knowledge items should strengthen originality and editorial trust
